Fuel systems

Thermal Engineering-II · DI05019021 · Carburetion · Pump · MPFI · CRDI

What you should be able to do

  • State the job of a fuel system: correct air–fuel mixture at the right time and place.
  • Explain simple carburetion (venturi + jet).
  • Say why a fuel pump is needed.
  • Compare MPFI (petrol) with CRDI (diesel) in one table.

2-minute idea

Petrol must mix with air before the spark. Diesel is injected into hot compressed air near the end of compression. That is why SI and CI fuel hardware look different.

Stoichiometric A/F for petrol is about 14.7 : 1 (14.7 kg air per 1 kg fuel). Richer (more fuel) helps starting and acceleration. Leaner (more air) helps economy, but too lean misfires.

Fuel pump — why it exists

      
The tank sits lower than the engine on most vehicles. Gravity cannot be trusted. A pump lifts fuel and feeds a steady pressure to the carburetor float chamber or to the injection rail.

      
    
        
  • Mechanical pump — cam-driven diaphragm (older petrol cars).
    • 
        
  • Electric pump — in or near the tank (MPFI).
    • 
        
  • High-pressure pump — feeds the common rail in CRDI. A separate low-pressure pump first brings fuel from the tank.
    • 
      

    


    

      
MPFI vs CRDI in one sentence each

      
MPFI: one injector per cylinder sprays petrol into the inlet port; air and fuel mix before the valve; spark ignites the mix.

      
CRDI: a shared high-pressure rail feeds injectors that spray diesel into the cylinder at the end of compression; hot air ignites the spray. No spark plug.

If you still don’t get it

      
    
        
  • Mistake: “CRDI is for petrol.” — Common Rail Direct Injection in this syllabus is the diesel system.
    • 
        
  • Mistake: “MPFI injects into the cylinder like diesel.” — MPFI is port injection, before the inlet valve.
    • 
        
  • Mistake: “Carburetor works at 1000 bar.” — Carburetion uses venturi suction, not a high-pressure rail.
    • 
      

      
Extra: GDI (petrol direct injection) exists on some cars, but GTU Unit 1.3 asks carburetion, pump, MPFI, CRDI — stay with those four names in the exam.
