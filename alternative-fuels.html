Alternative fuels

Thermal Engineering-II · DI05019021 · Alcohols · H₂ · Biofuel · CNG · LPG

What you should be able to do

  • Name why we look beyond petrol and diesel (reserve, cost, smoke, CO₂).
  • Give one gain and one problem for alcohol, hydrogen, biofuel, CNG and LPG.
  • Sketch the extra hardware for CNG / LPG on a vehicle (tank, PRV / vaporizer, piping).

2-minute idea

Petrol and diesel are liquid hydrocarbons from crude oil. An alternative fuel is anything else an IC engine can burn with some kit change: alcohols, biogas / biodiesel, CNG, LPG, hydrogen.

GTU wants properties + the supply kit on the vehicle, not a chemistry thesis.

CNG / LPG kit on the vehicle

CNG: cylinder (~200 bar) → high-pressure pipe →
filter → pressure reducer (PRV) → mixer / injector → engine

LPG: tank (liquid) → shut-off valve → vaporizer →
mixer / injector → engine

Exam words: PRV (pressure reducing valve) for CNG, vaporizer for LPG, plus leak-proof piping and a change-over switch if the vehicle is bi-fuel (petrol + gas).

5 practice questions

If you still don’t get it

  • Mistake: “CNG and LPG are the same gas.” — CNG is mostly methane, stored as gas at high pressure. LPG is propane/butane, stored as liquid at low pressure.
  • Mistake: “Hydrogen has no storage problem.” — Energy per litre is the hard part, not energy per kg.
  • Mistake: “Alcohol has a higher calorific value than petrol.” — It is lower. You need more litres for the same km.
