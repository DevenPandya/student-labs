Ignition system

Thermal Engineering-II · DI05019021 · Battery-coil · Magneto · Electronic

What you should be able to do

  • Say why only SI (petrol) engines need a spark, and CI (diesel) does not.
  • Sketch the battery-coil path: battery → ignition switch → coil → distributor / ECU → plug.
  • Compare battery-coil, magneto, and electronic ignition.
  • Explain spark advance in two lines.

2-minute idea

Petrol mix will not light itself at the right instant. A spark of several thousand volts jumps the plug gap near the end of compression.

The coil is a step-up transformer: 12 V in the primary, a sudden break of current, a high-voltage pulse in the secondary. Diesel engines skip this — hot compressed air lights the spray.

If you still don’t get it

      
    
        
  • Mistake: “Diesel engines use spark plugs.” — CI uses compression ignition. Glow plugs only help cold starting; they are not the spark system.
    • 
        
  • Mistake: “Spark at TDC always.” — Actual spark is a few degrees before TDC (advance).
    • 
        
  • Mistake: “Magneto needs a charged car battery.” — Magneto makes its own current from rotation.
